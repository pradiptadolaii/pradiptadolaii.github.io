A THREE-STEP WALKTHROUGH

From image to insight.

  1. 01
    Unlock the workspace

    Open the protected dashboard and enter the password shared by Pradipta.

  2. 02
    Choose a research mode

    Use Synthetic phantom → Run pipeline for the browser demonstration. For the model workflow, select images under Live MRI Research Analysis.

  3. 03
    Inspect the output

    Click Analyze MRI Images to review image previews and four category scores. Process up to 20 images sequentially, with a 10 MB limit per image.

Synthetic-pipeline metrics and ResNet18 scores belong to separate workflows. Model scores are uncalibrated outputs, not clinical probabilities.
